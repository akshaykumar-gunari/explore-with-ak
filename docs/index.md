## How this is organised

This is not a static resource — it's a **living knowledge system**.

Notes here evolve continuously as my understanding deepens. Concepts are revisited, refined, and sometimes completely reworked.

Each topic typically follows a simple pattern:

- Understand the **core concept**
- Break it down into **first principles**
- Reinforce it through **examples or implementation**
- Document it clearly for future reference

Think of this less like a polished textbook and more like an **open engineering notebook** — raw, evolving, and honest.

> *"The best way to learn is to explain it to someone else."*
